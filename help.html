YOUR BMI IS JUST THE BEGINNING

BMI can provide a useful starting point for understanding weight-related health risks. However, it does not measure body fat directly or account for factors such as muscle mass, body composition and individual health conditions. Your overall health depends on many factors, including nutrition, physical activity, sleep and mental well-being.
