Welcome to SDG-3

This is a student project aiming to support UN Sustainable Development Goal 3: Good Health and Well-being. Discover practical ways to build healthier habits and create a better balance in everyday life.

The definition of good health and well-being

Health is the foundation for everything else we do. So what does Good Health actually mean?

"Health is a state of complete physical, mental and social well-being and not merely the absence of disease or infirmity."

(Source: WHO – Constitution)

Having a good health is about more than avoiding illness. It means looking after your body, your mind and your relationships. When we sleep well, eat well, stay active and have people to lean on, we have more energy to study, work and enjoy life. Small habits built early can protect our health for years to come.

YOUR HEALTH MATTERS

Many health problems can be solved through awareness, prevention and access to appropriate healthcare. Building healthy habits today can help improve quality of life in the future. Therefore, it is important to take action and take good care of both your mental and physical health right now!

SMALL HABITS, BIG DIFFERENCES

A healthier lifestyle does not require dramatic changes. Regular physical activity, balanced nutrition, sufficient sleep and time to relax can all contribute to better well-being.
Start with small changes step-by-step and build routines that work for your everyday life.

THE STATE OF OUR HEALTH

How healthy are we really? Behind our daily routines are patterns that reveal serious challenges facing communities around the world.
From mental health concerns and physical inactivity to lifestyle-related health risks, understanding these trends can help us recognise where change is needed.
Let's take a closer look at what the latest health research tells us.

Three pillars of well-being

Mental Health

Understand your emotions, manage stress and build healthy relationships. Your mental well-being matters just as much as your physical health.

Physical Health

Keep your body healthy through regular movement, balanced nutrition, preventive healthcare and sufficient rest.

Healthy Lifestyle

Create sustainable routines around sleep, social connections, recreation and everyday choices.

Good health and well-being are essential for individuals and communities. However, access to healthcare, disease prevention and mental health support remains a challenge in many parts of the world.
The United Nations recognises this through Sustainable Development Goal 3 (SDG 3), which aims to ensure healthy lives and promote well-being for everyone at every age by 2030.
Achieving this goal requires awareness, prevention, accessible healthcare and collective action.
